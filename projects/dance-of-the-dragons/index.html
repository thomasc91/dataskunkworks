The Dying of the Dragons

In 129 AC House Targaryen held twenty dragons and two heirs with a claim to them. The war between the heirs spent the dragons on itself. Scroll, and each dragon flies the war's routes at its relative size, the two courts move and fall around them, and every death leaves a mark on the land.

Sources

Events, riders, deaths, places and sizes follow A Wiki of Ice and Fire (CC BY-SA 3.0), checked page by page. The summaries are original. Where the wiki's sources disagree, the text says so. Dragon sizes honour the ratios the wiki states (Vhagar five times Arrax, Caraxes half of Vhagar, Vermithor three times Tessarion) and are otherwise estimates, marked as such in the roll below.

How it's drawn

Everything is drawn live in plain JavaScript on a canvas, by the hand-drawn engine from Nightingale, redrawn: seeded noise, a pen line with pressure, washes that pool at their edges. The map is a schematic drawn from scratch from the wiki's placement statements; the dragons are articulated rigs seen from above, their bodies laid along their flight paths. Everything on the stage is a function of the scroll position, so scrolling back rewinds it. Turn on the notes to see the clean geometry underneath.
