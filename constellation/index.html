Constellation

A galaxy of 101 nodes: data at the core, then departments, the skills their agents use, and the outputs those skills produce.

What you're looking at

The centre is a company knowledge base, ringed by six data sources and their tables. Seven departments orbit it, each branching into agent skills that end in outputs. Hover a department to light up its branch, click one to fly in and fan it out, and scroll or use the buttons to zoom.

How it was made

A single file of D3 v7. A seeded random walk shapes the branches, and the force layout is settled before anything is drawn, so the map comes out the same on every load. The departments and skills are illustrative and describe no real company. Built with an AI agent in August 2026.
