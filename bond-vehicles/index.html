2025 / D3 / Scrollytelling

Every James Bond vehicle

492 scenes and about 14 hours of Bond behind the wheel, in the cockpit or at the helm. Scroll, and the scenes regroup to answer a new question at each step.

What you're looking at

Each circle is one scene in a vehicle, sized by how long it lasts and coloured by air, land or sea. As you scroll, the circles regroup: by element, by actor, by Bond's most-used vehicles and by class. Hover a circle for the film, the vehicle and the minutes on screen.

What the data says

The Spy Who Loved Me (1977) spends 53% of its running time in vehicles, the most of any film, while Dr. No spends 9%. The 1964 Aston Martin DB5 turns up in 22 scenes across 8 films, including four of Daniel Craig's five.

How it was made

D3 with a force layout that sends every circle to a new target at each scroll step, on Cuthbert Chow's scroller. The scene data was provided by Koen Van den Eeckhout.
