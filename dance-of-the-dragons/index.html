The Dying of the Dragons

Twenty dragons flew over Westeros at the start of 129 AC. Two years of civil war later, four were left. Scroll through the war and watch it happen.

What you're looking at

A map of the war's theatre in ink and wash. Twenty-one dragons fly the war's routes at their relative sizes, and 37 people move, change sides and die across 14 steps. Scroll or use the arrow keys to move through the story, hover any dragon, person or place for its fate, and press M for the maester's notes.

The data

Every event, rider, death, place and size follows A Wiki of Ice and Fire, checked page by page. Sizes keep the ratios the wiki gives, so Vhagar is five times the length of Arrax. Of the 37 people on the map, 25 die during the war.

How it was made

Every line comes from a small hand-drawn canvas engine, first written for a redraw of Florence Nightingale's rose diagram, with no libraries. Built with Claude in September 2026. It's a non-commercial fan work, not affiliated with George R. R. Martin or HBO, and it uses no imagery from the television series.
