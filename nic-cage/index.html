Nicolas Cage's film career

What you're looking at

The same chart as Tom Cruise's film career, on a much longer filmography. Each wedge's angle is the worldwide box office, and its length and colour are the Rotten Tomatoes score. Hover a wedge to lift it out. The face in the middle follows your cursor.

What the data says

The Croods (2013) is his biggest film, at $573M. Pig (2021) and Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse share his best score, 97%, while Grand Isle (2019) scored 0%. His films average 49% with critics, against 66% for Cruise's.

How it was made

D3's pie layout with an outer radius that changes from wedge to wedge. Two films with no Rotten Tomatoes score yet are left out.
