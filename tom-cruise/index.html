Tom Cruise's film career

46 films from 1981 to 2023, laid out clockwise in release order. Hover a wedge for the film, its score and its takings.

Open full screen View the code on GitHub ↗

What you're looking at

Each wedge is a film. Its angle is the worldwide box office, and its length and colour are the Rotten Tomatoes score, from red for the worst reviewed to blue for the best. Hover a wedge to lift it out and read its numbers. The face in the middle follows your cursor.

What the data says

Top Gun: Maverick (2022) made $1.48bn on its own. Mission: Impossible - Fallout has his best score, 97%, and Cocktail (1988) his worst, 9%. His films average 66% with critics.

How it was made

D3's pie layout with an outer radius that changes from wedge to wedge, so every wedge carries two numbers at once.
